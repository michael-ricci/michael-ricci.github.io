About Me

Who I am and what drives me

My name is Michael Ricci, and I am a student at Syracuse University with a strong interest in sports, media, business, and law. I am especially interested in careers that involve communication, persuasion, negotiation, and relationship-building.

A major part of my experience has come from working in athlete branding and NIL-related spaces, where I have learned how to pitch ideas, build partnerships, and think strategically about personal brands. I enjoy being in environments where I can connect with people, solve problems, and help create opportunities.

Beyond sports business, I also have a background in journalism, writing, and communications. Those experiences have helped me become a stronger storyteller and a more effective communicator. I am driven by ambition, competition, and the goal of building a career with real long-term upside.
